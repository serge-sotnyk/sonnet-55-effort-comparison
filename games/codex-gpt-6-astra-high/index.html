THE FIELD GUIDE

A crown is earned.

01 · Build an economy

Select villagers, then right-click trees, berry bushes, gold or stone. Build farms for a lasting food supply. Resources are delivered automatically. Houses increase your population limit.

02 · Grow your settlement

Select the Town Center to train villagers. Select a villager to place buildings. More builders finish construction faster. Build a Barracks to train infantry.

03 · Enter a new age

Use “Advance age” at the top left. Feudal Age unlocks archers and upgrades. Castle Age unlocks cavalry, castles and siege engines. Imperial Age unlocks long-range trebuchets. Advancing strengthens your army.

04 · Defeat House Raven

Find the rival in the northeast. Select soldiers and right-click enemies to attack. Use Attack move to fight along a route. Destroy their Town Center and military buildings to win.

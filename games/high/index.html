How to Play

Goal

Destroy all enemy buildings. Gather resources, advance through four ages, build an army and break the rival's walls.

Economy

Select villagers and right-click trees, berries, mines, deer or farms to gather. Villagers return resources to a Town Center, Mill, Lumber Camp or Mining Camp — build camps near resources. Houses raise your population cap.

Advance

Select the Town Center to advance ages. Each age needs specific buildings and unlocks units, upgrades and stronger architecture.

Combat

Spearmen counter cavalry, archers beat infantry, skirmishers beat archers, cavalry rides down archers. Rams smash buildings, monks heal and convert, towers and garrisoned Town Centers fire arrows.

Mouse

Left click / drag: select · double-click: select same type
Right click: move, attack, gather, build, repair, garrison, or set rally point
Shift+click: add to selection · queue orders · queue 5 units
Wheel: zoom · Middle drag: pan · edge scrolling

Keyboard

Arrows: scroll · Q W E R T / A S D F G / Z X C V B: command card slots
A: attack-move · S: stop · Del: delete · Esc: cancel / menu
. idle villager · , idle military · H Town Center · Space last alert
Ctrl+1…9 set group · 1…9 recall · + / - game speed · P pause · M music

Tips

Keep producing villagers. Build houses before you hit the cap. Wall your base, garrison villagers when raided, and upgrade at the Blacksmith.
